Hag is a cursed race albeit one of the rarest types of cursed race for it doesn't spread well enough. Hags are also a cursed race not tied to any deity but was created by mortals. The Hag cursed is obtained in two ways; First, by drinking the [[Hag's Brew]], a conconcation involving eating an immortal crow that is only immortal while in the brew.
The second, is by the [[Hag's Ritual]], being cursed for the Ritual by a Hag and escape before the ritual is completed.
Hags can become extremely powerful depending on how many times they've ascended through the Hag's Ritual. Newly created Hags are quite powerful but suffer from debilitating body effects such as growing feathers in wrong places, thinning of the bones, organ failures, beaked lips, growing extra limbs—including heads, curved spine, brittle bones, rotting skin and more. Due to their debilitating sicknesses, they are extremely skittish and rely on illusions during combat.
Upon their first ascendancy, Hags are able to transform into their victims looks and it's past looks. This means they could obtain the visual look of a child and as the years pass, they have access to older looks including the its younger counterpart. This allows them to blend in and even go undetected by powerful city wards. Only other Hags, powerful [[Witch|Witches]], and [[Illusionist|Illusionists]] may be able to see past the cursed illusion.
As a Hag age physically and become older, they're mind become sharper while their body withers and deteriorate. They begin to lose bodily passives while gaining acquirable long lasting buffs such as [[Regeneration]], [[En Stamina]], [[En Vitality]], etc.  Despite the aging, they tend to live long past their original race's life span. Kinan Hags can sometimes lives long past 100 years while being ridden to a cot in immense pain in the middle of nowhere. Few Hags will perfect Astral Project in order to overcome this and lure young girls into a trap in hopes of stealing their bodies through the Hag's Ritual.
