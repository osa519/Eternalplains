# Clause 1
## Section 1
The Adventurer's Contract is a Blood Contract which may result in Bondage to the Guild for no more than 10 years. Like all Blood Contract, the severity of penalities against the rules and agreement of this Contract varies.
## Section 5
All contracted Adventurers must accept all payments from the Guild. 
# Clause 4
## Section 1
Adventurers participating in Escorts and Delivery requests must account for Collateral Damage and Collateral Destruction

Collateral Damage is as follows.
* Death count resulting more than 35% of the escortees.
* Damage or lost to escortees property resulting more than 1,000P and less then 10,000P.
* Lost Products resulting more than 35% of items meant to be delivered or Lost Products resulting more than 1,000P
* Damaged products resulting in the delivered item unusable or in an unsatisfactory state.

Collateral Destruction is as follows.
* Death count resulting more than 35% of the escortees due to direct actions of the Adventurers.
	* Direct Actions includes but not limited to, poisoning, murdering, mutilating, harming, or attacking escortees or abandoning escortees in the lack of danger.
* Any Collateral Damage or Collateral Destruction done to escortees or properties of escortees of VIP status
* Damage or lost to escortees property resulting more than 10,000P.
* Lost Products resulting more than 10,000P

## Section 2
Adventurers are forgiven on their first Collateral Damage and are paid in full. This only apply to Adventurers on their first contract. Adventurers with voided or nulled contract on their record will not be entilted to this Section.

Adventurers are only forgiven on Collateral Destrcution if Collateral Destruction was in response due to the actions of the escortees. Examples but not limited to, attacked by the escortee, led into danger three Tiers above what was specified on quest, or led into a Known War Zone.
Acts of Overlords, Dragons, Kings, Weather, and Ents may also allow forgiveness on Collateral Destruction.
## Section 3
Adventurers on their first unforgiven Collateral Damage within one year are penalized. 

First Unforgiven Collateral Damage Penalty within one year is as follows.
* Can no longer accept Escort or Delivery Quests for one month.
* Recieve a 20% cut on all quests for six months
* First Unforgiven Collateral Penality is marked on Adventurer's Record for one year.

Second Unforgiven Collateral Damage Penalty within one year is as follows.
* Can no longer accept Escort or Deliver Quests for six months.
* Recieve a 40% cut on all quests for one year.
* Second Unforgiven Collateral Penality is marked on Adventurer's Record for one year.

Third Unforgiven Collateral Damage Penalty within one year is as follows.
* Can no longer accept Escort or Deliver Quests for One year.
* Recieve a 50% cut on all quests for Two years.
* Third Unforgiven Collateral Penality is marked on Adventurer's Record for one year.

Fourth Unforgiven Collateral Damage Penalty within one year is as follows.
* Can no longer accept Escort or Deliver Quests until approved by a Guild Leader.
* Recieve a 75% cut on all quests for one year.
* Can only accept Stake and Claims and Scout quests.
* Fourth Unforgiven Collateral Penality is marked on Adventurer's Record for two years.

Adventurers on any Collateral Destruction are penalized as follows.
* Adventurers are now in bondage to the Guild for the severity of years as stated in Clause 1, Section 1
* While in bondage, the Guild Leader residing over quest regarding the Collateral Destruction must do one of the following to the Adventurer
	* Imprisonment
	* Indentured Servitude
	* Severe Adventurer's Contract immediately but must also place the Adventurer into the hands of the Nation's Authority.
* After Bondage to the Guild, the Adventurer is placed into the hands of the Nation's Authority for applicable Crimes listed under Collateral Destruction.
