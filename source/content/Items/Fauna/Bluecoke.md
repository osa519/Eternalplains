Bluecoke are small bushy flowers that grows in spotted blue mounds. They grow in mountainous and cave settings. They can be refined and grounded whole into bluecoke powder used for Alchemy. They're used in:
* Antidotes
* Potions
	* Chill Resistance
	* En Ice Casting
* Acids

They can also be refined into [[Blue Sap]](Blue Xylem) and [[Blue Gel]](Blue Phloem)
[[Blue Sap]] is used in:
* Acids
* Polishing
* Cleaning Materials

While [[Blue Gel]] is used in:
* Potions
	* Mana Regen
	* Night Vision
	* Thermal Vision
* Enchanting
	* Chill Resistance
	* Freeze Resistance
	* Rust Resistance
* Rum
	* [[Blueno's Spiced Bull]] 
	* [[Burning Coke Rum]]
* Food Spicer
