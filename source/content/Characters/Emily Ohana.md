# Description
Sixty-nine but looks sixteen. She had bright orange skin with yellow leaf shape patterns flowing down her body. With her hood pulled down, snow damped her vibrant short red hair, and as she stomped through the snow, her red lips smiled, and her bright red eyes twinkled at me.
## History
She is the Captain of the 2nd Redead Squadron.

Born in the late state of [[Dark Valley]], she cursed to become a [[Hag Daughter]] that was saved a bit late by [[Lorenzo Nichols|Lorenzo]] who was a Squad Leader at the time. She was rejected by her family and taken in by Lorenzo. Redead leadership wanted her killed after finding out she had the Hag's Curse. After finding out they wanted her killed, she ran away. She was saved again by Lorenzo and turned into a [[Vampire]]. Lorenzo secretly took care of her and trained her until she learned [[Transfiguration]].

Emily can break her transfiguration through a chant “Hey Loki, I could use some help.” Forcing her to be engulfed in mana and regeneration. She then must ask her opponent or an ally, “In the deep, she cooks a stew, Of smells, and tastes, a special brew! She is very pretty, be sure to tell, Or you'll join the many, who now here dwell. What am I” If they answer correctly, Emily would be injured toward the point she almost dies. After three minutes, Emily can answer the question herself and revert back to her hag form. If the ally or enemy says anything or gets it wrong, Emily could answer the question.
