# Description
Bulging with muscles, with dark purple skin and black ring patterns around her limbs. She had auburn-colored waist-length hair that was tightly braided and glared at me with red pupils. Fifty-seven years old. Has a thing for the number 250. Titles - The Berserker, The Black Diamond, The Raging Seventh, The Hero of North Star.
## History
 She lost her father as he left to fight the war against giants. She was raised by her grandfather, a former soldier who grew up after the fall of the Ice Kingdom. He sent her off to train in Reh Bekah of Sante June. But these younglins were pitted against Giants. That was where she met the orphan Yuna and protected her as they were lost within the Giant Forest. Yuna healed her wounds as they navigated the lands for nearly two years until they found a famous and elderly broken Swordmaster Gildaheart Shadowkiller (a nuan/kinan who had survived running into a titan) Yuna healed him and he took both of them back to train them personally. (Eldritch and Yuna were accidentally heading deeper into giants territory)
No one believed Gilda about the appearance of the titan. During the height of the Giant War, news about a titan spread across the lands. Eldritch, already hardened by fighting giants for several years, was ready to prove her worth with Yuna at her side. As the battle raged, the last remaining contenders were seven fighters from around the nations, including Eldritch 7th, Yuna 8th, Ignis 2nd, The Black Knight 1st, Gildaheart 4th, Sirus 3rd(Force User Ill Lands), Brock 5th, the Haymaker(Monk/healer Raisin Plateau), Sarumonin D. Angel 6th, and the Titan, Guer Reh, the Calamity.
When returning to North Star, a squad of three giants were sieging. Now partnered with Joshua, the three of them after effortlessly slayed and the others fled, giving her the title of Hero of North Star. Once entering North Star, everyone, including her grandfather bowed to Astrea. After her grandfather passed away, she revoked being part of North Star for it was not the same anymore.
