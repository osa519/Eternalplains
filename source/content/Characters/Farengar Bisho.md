# Description
He wore black robes and carried an oak beat-up staff with a wand stashed snuggly in his black waistband. White eyes. His thick black hair bleed out the sides of his hood and the flickering light revealed his black skin patterned with thin silver lines that ran down his body. 37yo. He’s actually a lady and became a man to protect his mother from abusive men in the earth kingdom. Struck a deal to keep his mother and younger adoptive brother, Olaf (former slave with one eye) safe with Sarumonin to infiltrate The Iron Knights. If he dies during an adventure, money proceeds will go to his mother.

## History
