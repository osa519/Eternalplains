# Description
Kinan - Old Gent with pink-orange wrinkled face with straight silver hair and silver eyes. Orange limbs and pink fingers.

White Arachne - As its name suggested, she has a bright white feminine body, but unlike the broodmothers, the white arachne has vulnerable soft skin across its body and no chitin or carapace. Instead, dense carapace covered the four thick tentacle-like limbs protruding from out her back. The limbs ends in five flexible blunt limbs seemingly dense as steel. She took a step forward, her white legs smooth and glistened in the fiery light. Two pairs of arms were clasped before her torso and petite bosoms. Her hair was thick wiggly strands upon her head that fell down to the base of her neck and ended in wide beady eyes. Those eyes and the eyes on her soft face had hieroglyphic symbols instead of pupils at their center and with each blink, the symbols changed. Her [[School of Magic]] is Light.

Abimelech Goodgent was an old [[Cleric]] and [[Scholar]] of the former [[College of Emberfall]].
