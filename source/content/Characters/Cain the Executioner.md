# Description
Short auburn hair, pitch black eyes, darky gray skin patterned with symettrical red triangular patterns.
## History
Cain was born into a strong loving family yet truthful family. He was considered an accident like his brother, [[Adam Battleheart]] before him. His parents were enlisted while Adam was reserved to guard duty due to his parents having him. Adam was annoyed of taking care of his parents mistake. Cain knew it was because Adam was stronger then him and strived to garner strength. When Adam insulted his strength, the two fought twice. The first time, Adam spared Cain's life when Cain was only eight years old.
By twelve years old, Cain perfected his [[Cast Fire|Fire]] into a condense beam after being fascinated by [[Blasters]] and when Adam insulted Cain again, Cain killed him.
Cain began to take care of himself, lied to his parents and officials about his brother's death. The [[Warder|Warders]] imprioned him because they knew the truth all along. During his sentence, Cain learned and read propaganda about [[The Fire Kingdom||Fire Kingdom's]] past notable warriors and the strength of [[Fire King Tala]] in combat. Cain became more fascinated by [[Blasters]].
After 1 year, Cain was released and began the [[Squire Training]]. By 15 years old, Cain surpass expectation was immediatly promoted to an [[Instructor]] and given his own [[Spark Party|Spark]].
One year later, his parents joined the Rebels and Cain only figured that out when his Spark ran into his parents. His parents attempted to convinced him of the lies the [[Fire King Tala|King]] but Cain effortlessly killed them both.
