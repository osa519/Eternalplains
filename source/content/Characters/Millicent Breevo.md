# Description
She was short, with milky almond skin lined with pink dots resembling pupils down the sides of her face and neck. I noticed her muscles were profound—bulging through her tight leather sleeves. She had short pink-orange hair and was armed with two short swords at her waist.
## History
Endure raped by her father and uncle when she was ten, she eventually broke free and killed them both while they slept. She lived in the gentless home for a week before learning of the new [[Queen Cyn|Queen]] and the ability to become an [[Adventurers|Adventurer]]. Ladies throughout [[The Earth Kingdom]] quickly banded together in order to leave the nation.
