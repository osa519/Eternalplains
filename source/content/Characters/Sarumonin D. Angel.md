# Description
42 years old. He had pale skin with black feather patterns shadowing the edges of his eyes and corners of his lips. Small black diamond patterns spotted his noise and cheeks. His dome of black spiky hair were tipped with white. He was cloaked in a white and black designed duster robe with black fur boots and gloves.

Objective He created the guild in order to spread information. That was his ultimate goal. He is now focusing on what is causing the giants to attack Sante June.
## History
During his time as a wanderer, he ran into the black market and rose up the ranks to meet its leader Cyn's mother. The two both shared the passion for information and money. After fighting against a Titan, Saru had a strong passion and wanted to find out what other creatures lingered on the plains. He thus formed the Adventurers Guild with the help of the Black Market  and contracted Guild Officers throughout the Elemental Pennisula and now seeping into the other lands. Both he and Cyn disliked Murphy and were working secretly yet carefully to dismantle his power over the Earth Kingdom.
