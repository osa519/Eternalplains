# Description
Wears green robes. He had a head full of orange combed back hair and his pink face was riddled with thin blue lines. Drago's drunken mind was drawn in by the pattern and he noticed each line had tiny triangles—or were they arrows.

Tattooed on his back and along his arms is a complex hieroglyphic symbol of Pain Resistance and Body Control. (The symbolist who drew it took fourteen hours total with three hour long sessions each day) These symbols allow Creed to function despite the growing curse in his stomach but instead of dying in three years, he will perish within one year. 
## History
Creed made a vow to his faith in the Smiling God of the Sun, Solaris. He is currently on a three year pilgrimage through the Fire Kingdom to spread the message of Joy and remove the love of wrath amongst its people. He has a Blight Curse growing ever so slowly in his stomach and will eventually die of it in about three years.
