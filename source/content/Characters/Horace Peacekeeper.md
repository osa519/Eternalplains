# Description
Warrior/Knight, a beefy black haired bain with red ring patterns. Captain of the [[1st Division Redead Knights]]. Horace is a quiet father figure type of bain. He has always been a soft ruler growing up with his two younger brothers. He never really suffered like the other captain or knew what it is like to lose a loved one before their time. This made him extremely humble yet firm when leading others for he fears to ever experience that feeling.
