# Description
Cosmo has, silver eyes, a round face, blonde hair, and pale white skin patterned with yellow stripes along his cheeks.
## History
Cosmo could only recount that he was sold several times throughout the [[Raisin Plateau]] before being sold into the [[Sante June]]'s joint army at twelve. He spent three years with the Sante June army before he was recruited by [[Eldritch Wingheart|Eldritch]] at fifteen.
