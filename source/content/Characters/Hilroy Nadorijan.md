# Description
Pale white skin blotted with tiny brown arrow patterns. Hazel eyes and hair. I wore a marauder's red tail coat with yellow trimmings, a white long-sleeved shirt, black leather pants and studded high boots. Studded bracers protected my forearms and the gloves I wore were also reinforced with small steel beads. Along with my belt and supplies, a sheathe was tied around my waste harboring my expensive Steel Sword +1. 22yo.

Panuka’s mother dislikes him because he took her away from home. 

Afraid of heights. 
## History
Raised in a rich family of Farmers that was also a guild, was given money from his father after he left the family and was not allowed to return.
