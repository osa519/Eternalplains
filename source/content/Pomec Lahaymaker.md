# Description
Pomec Lahaymaker, holding the class Warrior and Brawler, was a forty-six year old gent with a stern deep bronze face. Flowing down his bronze face were white bronze lines, cutting through his eyes, meeting at the center of visible chest and spreading along the back side of his limbs. He was barefoot and wore black trousers that were torn at the ankles. Drapped over his torso was a scruffy brown coat tied at the waist and the sleeves rolled up to his forearms. 
He had black eyes and his black hair was braided and pulled back, leaving a few strands to dangle over his emotioneless face. But as his eyes fell on Mac, his rock chiseled expression melted away and was replaced by a soft caring smile.
